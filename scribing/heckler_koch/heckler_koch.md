# Names

## Wheel of Fire

The Individual True Name of this entity is "Jagat Chakra". There are
only 21 Ranks available. In other words, every Rank this Name is
raised by one individual, reduces someone else's Rank by the same
amount amount.

If the Spell of Name Change is known, and their Rank in the Generic
True Name 'Wheel of Fire' is Ranked to 10 or higher, then they may
assume this shape:

### The Wheel of Fire

The Namer rises about a foot above the ground, encircled by a
vertically aligned ring of barely visible white and blue flames.  In
this form, the Namer gains a Flywheel pool holding 1 Moment (+1 for
every 5 Ranks in the Generic True Name).  Momenti convert 1:1 to
hexes, and can be added to TMR in any direction.  Movement that
terminates with them unattached to the ground is generally followed by
aggressive acceleration toward the nearest surface below them.

The pool refreshes 1 Moment at a time at the beginning of the
following Pulse.

If the Namer is not in combat, they may increase their movement rate
over five minutes to 30 mph (+1 mph / Rank). They gain a bonus of
twice the Rank of the Generic True Name to any acrobatic or speed
based manoevre with the wheel.

Their Defence is increased by 2 (+3 for every even Rank in the Generic
True Name).

At Rank 20, they may perform an additional Defensive Withdraw, Retreat
or Non-Magical Pass Action except when Stunned. The actions are
resolved consecutively, in either order. The figure’s total movement
may not exceed their TMR.

### The Battle Form

The Namer may take a Pass Action and assume a Battle Form which also
affects their equipment. This changes the duration of the spell from
Concentration Max: 30 minutes (+30 minutes / Rank) to a standard
duration of 1 minute (+ 1 minute / Rank) (not requiring
Concentration), so must be Counterspelled to end it before duration
expires.

Based on their Rank in the Individual True Name, the change increases
their

- Size to 9, a 2 hex creature @ Rank 15, 3 @ Rank 20
- PS by 7 (+ 1 for every even Rank)
- EN by 8 (+ 1 for every even Rank)
- TMR by 1 (+ 1 @ Rank 10 & 20)
- Reduces each application of damage by 1 (+1 @ Rank 10 & 20) 

Bonuses to PS and EN from Strength of Stone or Waters of Strength and
the like do not stack, instead, the greatest effect applies.  They
gain a pool which holds 1 (+1 @ Rank 10 & 20) Fireheals.  Fireheal
causes the Namer's wounds to burst into flame, curing 1 (+1 for every
5 Ranks in the Individual True Name) DP so long as healing occurs
within a minute of receiving it.  It requires a Pass Action, and at
Rank 20, either a Pass Action or a Free Act but not both. The Fireheal
pool resets at dawn.

At Rank 10 in the Individual True Name and for each Rank thereafter,
the Namer can take a Free Act to ignite with white and blue flames
which inflict 1 point of magical fire damage to anything within a hex
of them.
