\section{The World}

\begin{multicols}{2}
  
In a multi-GM world many of the GMs have a world and or plane that
they proudly call their own, so you can expect to play anywhere or any
time. But Seagate is home and is shared by all GMs. Games are also
frequently set in nearby lands and these are the regions that players
get to know the most and use for character backgrounds.

\subsection{The Duchy of Carzala & the City of Seagate}

All characters are members of the Adventurer's Guild of Seagate. This
is located 5 miles south of Seagate, the capital of the Duchy of
Carzala.

Seagate is a port city on the northern bank of
the Sweetwater River and close to the sea.
Originally on a frontier between human
civilisation and wild lands, in the last few
decades it has become a prosperous trading
town, helped by the steady flow of cash and
contacts from the Adventurer's Guild. For ten
years between 798wk until 808wk it was on a
war footing against the Dark Circle.

The city is in three parts. Old Seagate is a free town, and is the old
walled town, cramped and dank and now home to the poor and the fishing
fleet. New Seagate is a bustling metropolis with explosive population
growth. It is walled to landward with docks for sea going vessels
downstream, river traffic upstream and the Bridge in the middle.
Between Old and New Seagate is Castle Chiltern, the ducal seat and the
houses and estates of the nobility. The population is large at
~18,000. A good third are recent newcomers, fleeing the influence of
the Dark Circle or just looking to get rich.

Across the river is a small dying village, once the home of the
ferrymen (but the new bridge has taken their livelihood), and five
minutes' ride down the road is the Adventurer's Guild enclave.

The rest of Carzala is typical coastal or river valley land scattered
with prosperous farming and fishing villages. Towns of note include
Slippery Rock with its coven of witches and Seacroft, home to many
active and retired guild members.

The culture of Carzala is early renaissance / fantasy with the
presence of our Guild and the Slippery Rock coven resulting in magic
being common and unusually respectable. The primary race is human but
common in the city are elves, dwarves and halflings. Orcs are more
rare, but not as reviled as in most human lands.

The climate is warm and coastal, not dissimilar to eastern Spain or
southern France.

South and west is the sea, northwest and north are other duchies,
baronies and small kingdoms collectively referred to by the older
scholars as ‘The Baronies’ and by the younger scholars as the Duchies.

Southeast are uninhabited rough lands and hills, rich in magic, exotic
wildlife and danger.  The Duchy of Carzala and Seagate are covered in
more detail in the 36 page Carzala Source book which can be downloaded
from the DQ Wiki.

\subsection{The Duchies & the Western Kingdom}

The Duchy of Carzala is one of a number of
small states filling the fertile lands between
the sea in the south and the long mountain
chain of the Alfheim Mountains in the north.
These are primarily human lands and once,
700 years ago, formed a kingdom. The
kingdom broke up, and different cultures and
languages separate the areas but travel, trade
and knowledge have increased enormously in
the last few generations. The area shares a
trade tongue 'Common'.

Two of the largest states, Aquila and Bowcourt, were joined by
marriage, and in 793 WK (more on dates later), they proclaimed their
joint heir to be King Ulric II of the (restored) Western
Kingdom. After a nasty war they absorbed some of their neighbours but
the larger or more distant states remained independent are still wary
of their neighbours and jealous of their borders.

The main power blocks (running vaguely east to west), and their
primary claim to fame are:
\begin{description}
\item[Carzala] the Adventurers Guild;

\item[Ranke] powerful and respected, except for one small town on the
  coast named Sanctuary, the most notorious thieves den in the known
  world;

\item[The Western Kingdom] military, expansionist,
and religious (Powers of Light) (lang: in
Bowcourt Lalange, in Aquila Folksprach);

\item[Mittelmarkhaupstadt] enormous wealth and fine wines (lang:
  Reichspiel)
  
\item[The Confederated Independent Duchies] including Brandenburg,
  Drakenburg, and Eltrandor. Fought the Western Kingdom and also much
  later sent part of their armies to fight the Dark Circle;

\item[Artzdorf/Flugelheim] fabulous horses;

\item[The Western Marches isolated and backward.

\end{description}

North of these and tucked in the foothills are many smaller states,
including Elfenburg and Borovia (known for elves), Gracht (known for
halflings), the Troll Kingdom, and areas controlled by dwarves or
orcs. Common is spoken throughout this region. The language is noted
only where Common is not the primary language.

\subsection{Alusia and the rest of the World}

The Duchies and the Western kingdom are only one part of the continent
of Alusia. Some 700 odd miles of mountain chain form their northern
border. Beyond is Alfheim, the Elven forest kingdom, stretching from
one end of the mountains to the other. Little is known about Alfheim
as the border has been closed to all but elves for millennia. West of
Alfhiem on the coast is the human Kingdom of Shorkyn. At the western
end of the mountains are dwarven cities, Granite Peak and HighCastle.

Westward again is Destiny, a mighty naval power whose ships dominate
the seas near the Duchies and beyond for many thousands of leagues.

Guild members are known to travel to the ends of the earth and beyond
and they come to know as fact places and people the general populace
only knows in stories.

\subsection{The Dates & Calendar System}

The Duchies currently use the Western Kingdom calendar, which started
with the founding of the original Western Kingdom.  There are 12
months of 30 days each divided into 4 seasons. Between each season is
a day of holiday being the first day of the season and belonging to no
month.

The year starts on Beltane, the first day of the summer season.  This
calendar has only recently come back into use. Prior to mid 797 WK we
used the normal calendar but with the date set to 9 months in the past
(so if the real date was 1 August 1996 the game date was 1 December
1995). This calendar started with the destruction of Penjarri so dates
are suffixed with AP (after Penjarri). To convert AP dates to WK
dates: for dates in Jan to Apr subtract 1200 from the year and add 8
months and for dates in May to Dec subtract 1199 from the year and
subtract 4 months.

Sunrise, sunset and tides are determined by
the GM and generally reflect reality.

\subsection{Religion and Greater Powers}

The dominant religion of the Western Kingdoms is that of the Western
Church.  Heretical scholars have pointed out that the religion’s
deities, the Powers of Light are in fact ascended elves who have taken
a custodial role over humans and other young races. The powers of
Light are concerned with order, civilisation and stability. Against
them are the Powers of Darkness, the demons.

Their chaotic, conflicting and often malevolent goals threaten peace
and harmony. Here and there, one can find pagan deities. These are
either ascended beings themselves or remnants of humanity’s original
gods.
