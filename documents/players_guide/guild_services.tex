\section{Guild Services}

\begin{multicols}{3}

This is a list of the services provided by the Guild and details of
some external services available.

\subsection{General Magical Services}

If a college is taught at the guild then members may also purchase
magical services from them. The abilities available are all those
taught by the guild and are at a minimum of Rank 6, (ie all talents,
all general knowledge spells and rituals, and any special knowledge
spells or rituals taught). Higher ranks are available for particularly
popular services and are detailed under the relevant college.

The costs are per attempt: and do not include material, if any.

\begin{tblr}{colspec={XQ[r,5em]},hline{1,Z}={lightgray}}
Talents                            & 100 sp \\
General Knowledge Spells           & 200 sp \\
General Knowledge Rituals          & 600 sp \\
Special Knowledge Spells           & 1,000 sp \\
Special Knowledge Rituals          & Price on asking \\
\end{tblr}

\subsection{Invested Items}

The Guild does not sell Invested Items.  Therefore, to assist members
in the purchase of such items elsewhere, we advise that the cost of
ingredients necessary to perform the investment ritual is as follows:
\textbf{Experience Multiple x Rank x Charges / 2}.

This formula is also used by the guild to value items. However, we
remind members that the chance of successfully performing the
investment ritual is not 100\%, and prices on the open market will
therefore reflect this.

\subsubsection{Resurrection}

Mistress Jessica’s BC normally is 88\% (rank + greater enchantment +
life aspect bonus) + patient’s normal full endurance (plus any patient
related modifiers as per the Healer skill.).

The minimum for a Rank 9 healer is 9\% and the maximum is 99\%.

As there is a limit on the amount of healing the Guild can supply,
post resurrection patients are usually only treated with a single heal
per day (5 Endurance points). Such healing is administered within an
hour of the resurrection which is normally performed as soon as
possible and thereafter at twenty four hour intervals. Thus it will
usually be several days before the patient is fully cured.

\subsection{Valuations, Divinations \& Sale of Unwanted Treasure}

When members return from adventure the guild assists with the treasure

items not wanted by members.  The charge is for this service is 2\% of
the value of each item and is in addition to any tax.

\subsubsection{Legal}

Guild members may obtain assistance with the drawing up of legal
documents or with conducting of law suits, both civil and
criminal. The Guild will especially make a concerted effort to help
where a member appears to being unjustly accused of a criminal
matter. The cost for legal services will depend on the exact
assistance provided.

\subsubsection{Financial Assistance}

The nature of their employment makes any member’s income extremely
variable. The guild will provide members with financial assistance in
the following manner: Purchase of Treasure: when the income from an
adventure is insufficient for a member to purchase their first choice
of treasure, then the guild will loan the member the money to do
so. They will only make this loan for one item and not if the member
has any other loan outstanding with the guild. The member must repay
the value of the loan plus 20\% within one year. The guild will hold
the item until it is paid for.

Guild Employment, part time: guild members with insufficient funds for
their upkeep can work for the guild and live in guild lodgings for
free. The member is expected to work every day from dawn until midday,
for no financial reward; but the afternoon is available for training.

Guild Employment, full time: guild members with insufficient funds for
their upkeep can work for the guild and live in guild lodgings for
free. The member is expected to work every day from dawn until dusk,
except Sunday where the afternoon is free. They will be paid a minimum
of 30sp per week. If a member has a skill at Rank 5 or higher that the
guild provides services in (eg healing), then the member may be
employed to use that skill and will be paid 10 + (rank x5) sp per
week.

Curse Removal: guild members sometimes require curse removal as a
result of training and have insufficient funds to pay for it. The
guild will loan the member funds for up to three curse removal
rituals. The member must repay the value of the loan plus 20\% within
one year.

\subsubsection{Residential Department Lodgings}

A Guild member can live at the Guild at a cost of 5sp per day. This
covers a single room and board of a good standard. Use of the
bathhouse (not including private rooms), laundry and dining hall is
included in the cost.  NB Members are advised to remember that on
Saturday mornings at 10 am sharp Blackfire removes all fleas.

\subsubsection{Lunar Bathhouse \& Laundry}

The laundry will wash and dry clothes for 1sp per sack. The old
bathhouse has been replaced by the new larger Lunar Bathhouse which
was opened in late 807wk. It is open 24 hours and located next to the
Courtiers’ training rooms. The bathhouse boasts under floor heating in
tasteful blue and green mosaics. The courtiers cleaned all warrior
types before entering the baths proper.  The Bathhouse has a total of
six public baths of differing temperatures (1cf), a total of five
steam rooms (2cf), and nine private bath rooms (14sp) – two are also
appointed with full time barman, servants and musicians (48sp).

\subsubsection{Dining Hall and Pub}

The dining hall provides food from one hour before dawn until one hour
after sunset.  There are two meals available, a light meal for 2
cf. or a large meal for 1 sp.  The Guild pub is open from around mid
morning until midnight, depending on business. The prices are standard
pub prices.

\subsubsection{Stable}

The stables are open from one hour before dawn to one hour after dusk.
Guild members can hire horses at a cost of 50 sp per week. This price
includes riding gear, (saddle, saddle blanket, saddlebags etc) and
insurance for the animal.  The stables will also look after members’
riding animals, at a cost of 5 sp per week.  However they decline to
look after carnivorous riding animals, as they have proved too much
trouble in the past.

\subsubsection{Guild Security}

Guild Security has responsibility for the safety of the Guild enclave
and people within it.  They guard the gates and walls, debrief
incoming members, investigate potential employers, guard the guild
valuables, and are answerable only to the Council.

\subsubsection{Debriefing}

This service is free and unavoidable.

\subsubsection{The Vault}

The guild vaults are a secure area that the guild uses for the storage
of unsafe magical items, its own valuables and members’ valuables.

A member may arrange to store mundane valuables in the vaults at a
cost of 2 sp per month per small chest (30 lbs or 500 oz
approximately).  These valuables are readily available to the member.

A member may arrange to store unsafe magical items in the vault for
free; however they will have to justify any later removal of the item.

\subsubsection{Cartography}

The guild holds a wide range of maps of Alusia and will make copies
for members.  This normally costs 200 sp per map, and takes ½ a
day. More detailed maps, or maps in a foreign style may take longer
(and cost more).

The library will buy new maps from adventurers. The maximum they will
pay is 1,000 sp for new, detailed maps of large areas, where the
adventurer can confirm the accuracy of the map. They pay lesser
amounts for maps with sparse detail, lack of accuracy, or for obscure
regions or of other planes.

\subsubsection{Research}

There are between 300 – 400 books and
scrolls in the Guild Library. It is especially
strong in the realms of magic and the social
world pertaining to the duchies and nearby
areas. It is rather poor on the realm of plants
except for local stuff. For philosophic

  Duchies and nearby cultures

\item[Research Rating 5 - 8] The Social World, field of history,
  politics, customs, heraldry etc.
\end{description}
