\section{Price List}

This is a list of goods and services that are readily available in
Seagate and their normal prices. Most goods are of average quality
(Rank 0).  Where prices are quoted for better quality goods these will
have been made at higher ranks as well as including better
ingredients.  Some goods have no weight quoted as single items have
negligible weight. Large quantities of these should be given the
nominal weight of 1/2 oz each.

\subsubsection{Armour}

Armour requires fitting to the wearer.  This takes between 1/2 an hour
to several days. See Section 30.2 Armour for the details.

Armour weights are calculated by multiplying the weight factor by the
racial factor. Females should subtract 0.5 from their racial factor.
