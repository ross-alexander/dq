\section{Cost of Living}

\begin{multicols}{2}
  
This is a guide to how much a character needs to pay to maintain a
certain standard of living. It is designed so you can choose a level
of lifestyle for the character and pay for it, without having to go
into the details every time you update your character’s finances.
Characters down on their luck can gain Financial Assistance from the
Guild.

\subsection{Living in Lodgings}

Characters who do not live at the guild can lodge in town in inns,
boarding houses and sometimes as a room or suite of rooms in someone’s
household. Accommodation, meals and laundry are included but they will
need to provide their own clothes to a certain standard.
