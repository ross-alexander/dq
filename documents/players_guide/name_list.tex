\subsection{Standard Names}

Members may learn the following names:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Any name on the list taught to beginning Namers
  \item  Any other entity in the Bestiary
  \item Any type of plant, animal, bird or fish commonly seen around or near Carzala (eg. cabbage
    or trout)
\end{itemize}
