\begin{enumerate}
\item
Commemorative days are in effect from dawn on the day stated, until
dawn of the day following.

\item
A Friday which falls on the 13th of a month is also a Dark holiday.

\item
All Dark days are also Necromantic Death Festival days, and all Light
days are Life Festival days.

\item
All the Saints days, including Michaelmas and All Souls Day, are low
mana days, while all Season days and those marked as Faerie days are
high mana.
\end{enumerate}
